How it works

From a blank page to a tracked search

Step 1

Get interviewed

Talk through your roles, or upload a resume. Every line is recorded in your own words.

Step 2

Check the fit

Paste a job description. Get a score, what you meet with evidence, and which gaps are real blockers.

Step 3

Get a resume

A tailored Word and Markdown resume where every bullet traces back to something you said.

Step 4

Track the search

Log applications and answers. The funnel shows where they turn into interviews, or stop.

The team

Five agents, one profile

You talk to one front door. It hands you to whichever specialist you need.

Interviewer

Learns your career

Asks what you built, for whom, and what changed. Pushes for numbers.

Intake

Reads the posting

Turns a pasted job description into a clean list of requirements.

Fit Analyst

Scores honestly

Separates stretch gaps from blockers, and cites evidence for each match.

Resume Tailor

Writes it down

One resume per job. A plain-code check rejects any claim it cannot trace.

Coach

Keeps the record

Logs every application and outcome, and draws the funnel above.

You

Own the data

Profile, jobs, reports and journal are plain data you can read and correct.

Rules that don't bend

No invented experience

A resume can only say what your profile says. If it is missing, the agent asks you first.

The profile is the truth

Agents do not keep private notes about you. Everything they use is in your profile.

You can read everything

If anything on file is something you never said, that is a bug worth reporting.
